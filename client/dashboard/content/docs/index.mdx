# MotionMesh Documentation

MotionMesh is a self-hosted video infrastructure platform — upload, store, transcode, stream, and embed video in your app in minutes. Backed by Go, FFmpeg, and S3-compatible storage, with a type-safe JavaScript/TypeScript SDK.

> **New in v0.5** — cost-based tiered pricing (Free / Pay-as-you-go / Pro / Scale) with server-enforced quotas, a rewritten usage & billing dashboard, and an interactive pricing estimator. See the [Changelog](/docs/changelog).
